How TaxFreely Calculates Your Tax

Progressive Bracket Calculation

Income tax is progressive — the more you earn, the higher the rate on each additional pound, dollar, or euro. TaxFreely applies each bracket's rate only to the income that falls within it, exactly as the tax authority does. The results screen shows every bracket that applies to your income, the amount taxed in each band, and the tax owed in that band.

Self-Employment Rates Separate From Employee Rates

In most countries, self-employed workers pay a higher social security contribution than employees because they pay both the employee and employer portions. TaxFreely shows the freelancer-specific rate rather than the employee rate, which is what most other calculators use by default. This difference can be several thousand dollars or pounds per year on a typical freelance income.

VAT and GST Registration Threshold Alerts

When your projected income approaches or exceeds the VAT or GST registration threshold for your country, TaxFreely shows a real-time alert. Missing the registration deadline is one of the most common and costly mistakes new freelancers make. The threshold is different in every country and is updated whenever tax laws change.

Quarterly Estimated Tax With Exact Due Dates

In countries where freelancers pay tax quarterly — including the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and many others — TaxFreely calculates the estimated payment for each quarter and shows the exact due date. Push notifications remind you seven days, three days, and one day before each deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TaxFreely free to use?

Yes. All core tax calculations across all 195 countries are free. No account or subscription is required. Optional Pro features are available as a one-time purchase.

Does TaxFreely work offline?

Yes. After the first load, TaxFreely works completely without internet. All tax data and calculation logic is stored on your device. Ads will not display while offline, but every calculation feature works without a connection.

Is TaxFreely only for freelancers?

No. TaxFreely calculates tax for freelancers, self-employed professionals, employees, contractors, and business owners. You select your employment type at the top of the calculator and the correct rates are applied automatically.

How accurate is the tax data?

TaxFreely uses official tax brackets and rates published by each country's tax authority. The AI Tax News feature alerts you to recent changes. If rates have changed since the last app update, you can enter custom rates manually. TaxFreely provides estimates for financial planning — always confirm your final tax liability with a qualified tax professional.

Is my financial data secure?

Yes. All calculations run locally on your device. Your income, deductions, and results are never sent to any server. TaxFreely does not have accounts, so there is no database of user financial data. The only data that optionally leaves your device is anonymous, aggregated, and stripped of all personal details — and only if you choose to opt in to help improve the country benchmarks feature.
